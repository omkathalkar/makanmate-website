Pune pulls in more students and first-job movers than almost any other city in India — and has some of the messiest, broker-heavy rental hunting to match. We're fixing it here first.

Why Pune, and why now

Every year, hundreds of thousands of people arrive in Pune — students joining Symbiosis, COEP, or Fergusson; engineers starting at Infosys or TCS in Hinjewadi; recent graduates chasing their first job in the IT corridor. They all need a place to stay, and they all face the same broken system.

Brokers who disappear after taking their fee. Photos that are three years old. Landlords who suddenly add conditions. And no one you know to tell you which areas are safe, which ones flood in monsoon, or where the nearest decent canteen is.

MakanMate isn't building for all of India at once. We're building neighbourhood by neighbourhood in Pune, getting every detail right before we go anywhere else.
