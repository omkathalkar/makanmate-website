What no one tells you before you move to Pune

The stuff that would've saved you two weeks of confusion.

🌧️ Monsoon matters more than you think

Pune gets heavy rainfall June–September. Areas near rivers (parts of Kothrud, Karve Nagar) can flood. Always ask about waterlogging history before signing. Avoid ground-floor PGs unless the building has good drainage.

🛺 Auto fares are negotiated, not metered

Most autos in Pune don't use meters outside the core city. Know the standard rate for your route before you get in — ask a local, not the auto driver. Rapido and Ola Auto are more reliable for short trips.

🔑 Deposit is usually 2–3 months

Standard security deposit in Pune is 2–3 months' rent. Anything above 3 months is unusual and worth questioning. Get every amount written in the agreement — verbal promises disappear fast.

🌡️ Weather is genuinely pleasant

Pune sits at 560m above sea level — noticeably cooler than Mumbai or Hyderabad. Summers are warm but not brutal. Winters (Nov–Jan) can get surprisingly cold at night. Pack a light jacket.

🧾 Agreement must be registered

Rental agreements over 11 months must be registered at the sub-registrar's office. Many landlords skip this — it protects them too. Unregistered agreements above 11 months aren't legally enforceable.

🍽️ Mess culture is a real thing

Many PG areas have affordable "mess" tiffin services — ₹60–100 per meal, monthly subscriptions available. Ask your PG owner for recommendations. A good mess nearby can save ₹3,000–5,000 a month vs. eating out.

Young professional & IT hubs

Hinjewadi

Pune's largest IT park — Infosys, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant all here. Demand for shared flats is constant and high. Traffic on the Hinjewadi–Wakad road is notoriously bad; living walkable to the park changes everything.
