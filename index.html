Questions people ask us

If yours isn't here, email contact@makanmate.in — we read every message during the pilot.

Is MakanMate free to use?

Yes, for seekers it's free during the Pune pilot. There's no brokerage fee, and no charge to browse or join the waitlist.

How do you actually verify listings?

Someone from our Pune team visits the property in person before it's published — checking the photos, the rent, and the distance claims match reality.

When will my area or city open up?

We're onboarding Pune neighbourhood by neighbourhood to keep verification tight, then expanding to other cities. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the moment your area's live.

I own a property — how do I list it?

Use the "List your property" option in the waitlist form below. We're onboarding a limited number of verified owners per neighbourhood first.

Do you only do PGs, or full flats too?

Both — PGs, shared flats, and independent 1BHK/2BHK rentals, depending on what's available and verified in your chosen area.
