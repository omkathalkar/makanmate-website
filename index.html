Everything in the app

Browse free, no sign-in

See every verified listing — photos, rent, safety score, distances — without creating an account. Sign in only when you want to chat or book.

Budget and location filters

Filter by budget (Under ₹5k / ₹5–8k / ₹8–12k / ₹12k+), near college or IT park, room type, food included, couple-friendly, and more.

Full listing detail

Photos, all room options with per-person rent, safety score breakdown, walk times to metro/college/grocery, and a 360° tour — all in one screen.

Save and compare

Shortlist rooms with one tap. Compare any two side by side on rent, safety score, distance to your office or college, and total move-in cost.

Book a visit

Pick a date and time slot. The owner confirms in the app. Reschedule or cancel anytime — no awkward phone calls needed.

Reserve a room

Pay a ₹500 token to hold your chosen room for 48 hours while you sort travel and paperwork. Refundable if you decide not to move in.

Pay rent in-app

Track due dates, pay rent directly, and get a digital receipt. No running to the bank or hunting for the owner's UPI ID each month.

Paperwork, all digital

Maharashtra registered leave and licence, police verification, ID via DigiLocker, and e-sign — all completed in the app. Government fees shown upfront.

Safety score and SOS

Every listing shows a 0–10 safety score: street lighting, police proximity, CCTV, women safety reports, fire safety. One-tap SOS calls your emergency contacts.

Add a property in 5 steps

Basics, rooms, features, location, photos. Done in under 10 minutes. Send photos by WhatsApp if you'd rather not type — we handle the listing.

Manage visits

New, upcoming and done tabs. Accept or decline visit requests, see who's coming, reschedule with one tap. No missed call confusion.

Rent and reminders

See who's paid and who's overdue at a glance. Send a WhatsApp payment reminder with one tap. Track rent history and deposits per tenant.

Bed map

Visual map of every bed and room. See which are occupied, note move-out dates, mark a bed as available the moment it frees up.

Analytics dashboard

Views, enquiries, visit conversions and average days-to-fill over 7 / 30 / 90 days. See why your listing ranks where it does and what improves it.

Tenant paperwork

Send ID check via DigiLocker, police verification link and leave and licence e-sign — all from the app on WhatsApp. Track which step each tenant is on.

Subscription plans

Free lists the property. Verified (₹299/mo) adds a trust badge and faster responses. Pro (₹799/mo) adds priority ranking and dedicated support.

360° VR tour

Walk slowly through the property with any phone. Stitched into a shareable 360° tour — send it on WhatsApp so tenants tour without travelling first.

WhatsApp assistant

Handle new enquiries, send rent reminders and update your listing entirely from WhatsApp — tap buttons and voice notes, no app needed for daily tasks.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

If yours isn't here, email contact@makanmate.in — we read every message during the pilot.

Is MakanMate free to use?

Yes, for seekers it's free during the Pune pilot. There's no brokerage fee, and no charge to browse or join the waitlist.

How do you actually verify listings?

Someone from our Pune team visits the property in person before it's published — checking the photos, the rent, and the distance claims match reality.

When will my area or city open up?

We're onboarding Pune neighbourhood by neighbourhood to keep verification tight, then expanding to other cities. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the moment your area's live.

I own a property — how do I list it?

Use the "List your property" option in the waitlist form below. We're onboarding a limited number of verified owners per neighbourhood first.

Do you only do PGs, or full flats too?

Both — PGs, shared flats, and independent 1BHK/2BHK rentals, depending on what's available and verified in your chosen area.
