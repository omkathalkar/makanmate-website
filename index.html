Chat with us
Is MakanMate really free for tenants?

Yes. Browsing, chatting with owners and booking visits are free. There's no brokerage and no fee to see contact details.

How do you verify a PG?

Someone from our Pune team visits in person and checks photos, rent and house rules. Each listing shows the date it was last verified.

What happens to the ₹5,000 token?

MakanMate holds it. It's adjusted in your deposit when you move in, and refunded in full if the owner cancels, the place doesn't match, or you cancel within 48 hours.

Can I see a place before I travel to Pune?

Yes. Book a live video tour on WhatsApp with the owner, and share the visit details with your family.

Why is the owner's number hidden?

Chats run through MakanMate so your number stays private too, and every conversation is on record if something goes wrong.
